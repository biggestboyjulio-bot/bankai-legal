This Privacy Policy describes how Bankai (“the application”) may handle information when you use its clip-management and upload features. The actual information handled depends on the features you enable and the services you connect.

1. Information the application may handle

Depending on your use of Bankai, this may include video files you select or create, application settings, connected-account authorization data, basic account information made available by a connected platform, and technical information about upload status or application operation.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide the features you choose, such as organizing clips, connecting an account, submitting uploads you initiate or configure, displaying upload status, and troubleshooting or maintaining the application.

Video files and settings may be stored on your device or in folders you configure. Information retained by a connected platform is handled under that platform’s policies. Review your application settings and local folders to understand what is stored on your device. Delete local files you no longer want to keep and disconnect accounts you no longer want linked.

4. Sharing and third-party services

Bankai may send information to a third-party platform when you connect to it or request an upload. That platform handles information it receives under its own privacy policy and terms. Do not connect an account or upload content unless you understand and agree to the relevant platform’s requirements.

5. Security

Reasonable care should be taken to protect your device and any account credentials or authorization tokens used by the application. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Your choices

You can stop using Bankai, disconnect a connected account through the applicable platform, and delete locally stored clips, settings, or other application files. You may also use the platform’s own privacy and data controls for information it holds.

7. Children's privacy

Bankai is not intended to be used in a way that violates applicable age requirements or the terms of connected platforms.
